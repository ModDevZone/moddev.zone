# Guide to Mod Dev Zone

## Staff Roles & Expectations
- **Managers** - Managers are the ones making all the top-level decisions, maintaining the infrastructure, watching over the community and staff, and dealing with general day to day management of the community.
- **Moderators** - Moderators are responsible for making sure the community behaves and runs smoothly. Our moderators handle giving out bans, kicks, timeouts and roles.
  They also help members with questions and issues they may have regarding the community or its members, and generally assist in keeping the community a safe and fun place to be.
- **Assistants** - Assistants help the moderators with their duties, think of assistants as jr. moderators.
  They can issue warnings, timeouts, kick users who continue to break the rules and assist with role management, should you need help then just ask!
